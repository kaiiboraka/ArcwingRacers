## Kael Losallion

**Element:** Sun / Light  
**Age:** 16  
**Role:** Prince of Losallia; Archon of Losa (Sun Goddess)  
**Combat:** Close-range sword fighter. Gains elemental Sun-side spells from boss crystals.

**Personality & Arc:** Confident, purpose-driven, oriented toward destiny and heroism. His arc is learning that what matters isn't fulfilling a grand purpose — it's the love of the people around him. Kael's rage comes from grief and loss; it slowly transforms into peace and acceptance. Learns that lifting those around you is how you lift the world.

**Baseline moveset:**
- 3-hit sword combo (ground)
- Single Air Slash (air)
- Wall-jump

**Unlock order (boss crystals → elemental spells):** Collects Sun-side elements (Light, Fire, Earth, Iron, Wood, Life) from elemental bosses in Acts 2–4.

**Key Details:**
- Biological son of King Simon Losallion and Viona Dawson.
- Raised as the Losallian prince; heir to the throne.
- Raised apart from Rina; the two meet during the game's events.
- Has a Sun Spot — a birthmark that marks him as Losa's Archon.
- Archon power triggers in a moment of intense emotional/physical stress (permanent mana boost, heightened senses, unlocks true elemental potential).

## Rina Dawson

**Element:** Moon / Shadow  
**Age:** 16  
**Role:** Archon of Ryla (Moon God)  
**Combat:** Ranged/arcane caster. Gains Moon-side elemental spells from boss crystals.

**Personality & Arc:** Selfish, driven by personal goals (finding her mother Viona), dismissive of others. Her arc is discovering empathy — that everyone she's looked down on has a life worth living, and that losing yourself in service to others is how you find yourself.

**Baseline moveset:**
- Fires 3 onscreen projectiles (ground)
- Jump

**Unlock order (boss crystals → elemental spells):** Collects Moon-side elements (Shadow, Water, Ice, Wind, Lightning, Death) from elemental bosses.

**Key Details:**
- Biological daughter of King Simon Losallion and Viona Dawson.
- Raised by adoptive grandmother Julianne Dawson (and adoptive uncle Garrett).
- Raised apart from Kael; the two meet during the game's events.
- Only keepsake: her mother Viona's magic tome (used to unlock new spells).
- Can glimpse the future of anything — except the moon (i.e., anything related to Ryla or Shadow is hidden from her foresight).
- Archon power triggers the same way as Kael's.

## Dual-Protagonist Structure

The game begins with a character select. The chosen character plays through the full campaign from their perspective. The unchosen character is a present, active force in the story — they simply operate off-screen during the chosen character's portions.

**Act structure:**
- **Act 1:** Each character has their own intro level track (Kael: K1 levels in Amolna; Rina: R1 levels in Amolna). Parallel, non-intersecting.
- **Acts 2–4:** Parallel tracks that occasionally cross. Characters pursue separate objectives but the consequences affect each other.
- **Acts 5–6:** Tracks converge. The twins are together for the conclusion.

**The unchosen twin's fate:**
- When Kael is chosen: Rina is captured after the Amolna attack, sent to the Prison Tower, and taught Arcane magic — making her a powerful but elementally-limited Arcane caster.
- When Rina is chosen: Kael escapes but takes a different path, becoming a spellsword who augments his attacks with Arcane and gains Arcane mobility (teleport), with no elemental breadth.

This asymmetry ensures both playthroughs feel mechanically and narratively distinct.

## Parameters

**Health (HP):** Refilled by Flasks (large, on-demand), enemy drops (small, in-combat), and Life-element spells (traded for mana). See Obsidian Vault → `ProjectFantasyX/Mechanics & Systems/Parameters/Health.md`.

**Mana (MP):** Generated by physical damage dealt and enemy/destructible drops. Spent on magic spells. See `game-design/gameplay/magic-combat-system.md`.

**Super Meter:** Charges via combat; expended for Super Attacks. Regen mechanic tied to Back slot gear. See Obsidian Vault → `ProjectFantasyX/Mechanics & Systems/Parameters/Super.md`.
