# Scripted Encounter: Enter the King's Palace

Also referred to as "Cameos" in source material — a way to bring nostalgic 80s parody moments into the game, and a hook to make players curious what other surprise endings exist.

## Overview

When the player reaches the King in the palace (see `world/locations/castle.md`'s "Defeating the King"), a short random scene plays out first — the King is occupied with something else entirely, unaware of the player's arrival — before the player interrupts and the King is eaten/defeated. Player loses control while the scene plays (see `Scripted Encounter` in `docs/agent-context/context-map.md`'s Key Vocabulary).

## Cameo Pool

Random scene, picked per visit:

- **Stargazer cameo** `[Thief]`: The King calls for the court astronomer. A Jack Horkheimer parody ("Stargazer") descends from a rainbow to eerie space music, delivers a star-gazing forecast, spots the player, and beams out: *"Always keep looking up!"* `[Ref: Jack Horkheimer, Star Hustler/Star Gazer (TV)]`
  - King: *"Ahh I love that guy, now I can sleep."* Turns to the player: *"eh? What's this? Guuuards!"*
  - Thief: *"Oh, you mean this guy?"* — tosses the Captain's giant helmet onto the floor (bounce/clink).
- **Bloodsport cameo**: Van Damme's Frank Dux character is fighting a Sumo opponent on a kumite mat in front of the throne. `[Ref: Bloodsport (1988)]`
  - King: *"Bottom one!"*
  - Dux shrugs, then delivers a Dim Mak "Hwaaaa" splits punch to the groin. (See `gameplay/player-characters/character-tokens.md`'s Kumite Champ entry for the Dim Mak ability elsewhere in the game.)
- **Princess Bride cameo** `[Viper]`: The King is confronted by a Spaniard parody: *"My name is Indigo Montana, you killed my father. Prepare to die."* `[Ref: The Princess Bride (1987)]`
  - The Viper swoops in and swallows the King, leaving the Spaniard speechless (shrug, looks around).
  - A small bald figure peeks out from behind the throne: *"Inconceivable!"*
- **Spaceballs / Neverending Story cameo**: The King is playing with action figures — Atreyu approaching the oracles, set on his treasure chest. `[Ref: Spaceballs (1987), The Neverending Story (1984)]` Shown from behind, looking at the King facing the chest; a guard later approaches from behind him.
  - King (playing): *"Oh no...don't start to doubt yourself...be confident."* *"Be confident!"* *"Run Atr—"*
  - Guard: *"Your highness!"*
  - King: *"WHAT??"*
  - Guard: *"You're needed at the gate."*
  - King: *"Knock on my door! KNOCK next time!"*
  - Guard: *"Yes, sir."*
  - King: *"Did you see anything?"*
  - Guard: *"No, sir. I didn't see you playing with your dolls again."*
  - King: *"Good!"*

## Cameo Rules

- Brief appearances only.
- Never used in advertisements.
- No music rips — either omit the music entirely or compose an original version in a similar style.

`[TBD]` Only one cameo above is explicitly tagged `[Thief]` and one `[Viper]` in source material; the rest don't specify which player type sees them — unclear whether they're shared across both or character-specific.
