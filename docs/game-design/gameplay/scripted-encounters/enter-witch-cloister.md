# Scripted Encounter: Enter the Witch's Cloister

Entering the neck of swamp water at the bottom of the Witch's enclosed swamp area (see `world/locations/swamp-entrance.md`) transitions the player into the **Swamp Maze** level — a separate, much larger play space than the small enclosed zone shown on the world map. See `world/levels/swamp-maze.md` for what's inside.

At the very start of the maze, the player is immediately engaged by a **Horned Viper** (see `gameplay/enemies/viper-guardian.md`). The player can either fight it directly, or bring captives as bait/distraction to slip past it.
