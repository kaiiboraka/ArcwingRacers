# Dark Lord — Boss Battle

Lil' Tom's unique Character Token quest (see `gameplay/player-characters/character-tokens.md`'s Lil' Tom entry — *Legend, 1985* inspired). Played solo, with no paired Viper — Tom enters this arena alone and vulnerable.

`[Renamed]` Referred to as "Darkness" in earlier source material — **Dark Lord** is the current name, kept distinct from the 1985 film's antagonist's exact name.

## Opening / Arena

The Dark Lord is a demonic lord of night, appearing as a reskinned **Viper**, encountered in a hellscape quest scene: fire-pit tiles, with a shaft of light falling from the ceiling.

## The Battle — Setup

Find the source of light (the ceiling shaft) and a set of movable shields that bounce/redirect that light around the arena. The Dark Lord treats an active beam as an impassable wall — he won't intentionally walk into one.

A sword is also findable in the room outside the encounter; swinging it deflects the Dark Lord's charge and protects you from being eaten, but it's purely **defensive** — it doesn't damage him, and each deflection costs you by growing him a tile.

## Win Method 1 — Light Ray

Reposition the shields so the beam intersects part of the Dark Lord's body.

- A beam slicing through his **head** kills him outright, provided the beam stays on him long enough — if he breaks contact too soon, he escapes and heals.
- A beam landing anywhere else along his body just shortens him by a tail segment instead: the cut tile regrows into a small tail stub, while the severed remainder decays and sinks into the ground.
- Because his total health scales with his (Viper) length, chopping segments down first makes a direct head-cut easier/more reliable.

The Opening Cutscene above demonstrates this same shield-positioning beat narratively (Tom does it once, scripted) before handing control to the player to do it for real.

This victory grants achievement: Another Dawn.

## Win Method 2 — Unicorn Horn + Mount

A unicorn horn is hidden in the level — a barely visible glint in one of the fire pools, spottable in the gap between fire jets. Thrown at the Dark Lord, it stuns him briefly, letting Tom mount and steer him directly, like a normal Viper. [Game mechanic = stand next to the sparkling horn and hit "steal" button]. Then anytime you're facing the viper, you have a new "throw action" button. If the stun wears off, you can try again and pick up the horn which bounces off his head and falls to the ground there.

Mounting overrides his usual beam-avoidance behavior, so the player can simply steer him into an active beam — his head hits the light first, killing him instantly in a piercing flash. The Dark Lord eventually thrashes free if you're too slow.

This win path grants a hidden achievement: Nothing More Magical.
