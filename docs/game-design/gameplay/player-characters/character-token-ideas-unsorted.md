## Note on Overlap

Some of these names already have a relic, consumable, or flavor quote attached in `loot/loot-types.md` (e.g., Knight Rider's Kitt's Watch relic — renamed from "Auto Watch" — Scooby-Doo's Sleuthy Snacks consumable, Star Trek's Badge Communicator) even though they have **no playable character** behind them. An item existing in the loot catalog does not promote the franchise to a Character Token — those are two separate, independently-tracked lists.

**Confirmed per user decision this session:** this also covers relics reclassified from "Character Token-Exclusive" to "General Loot Pool" in `loot/loot-types.md` (Kitt's Watch, Spy Glass Orb, Locksley Medallion) — moving a relic to the general pool because its franchise lacks a fleshed-out character does **not** promote that franchise to canonical status here either. Knight Rider, Labyrinth, and Robin Hood remain non-canonical name-only ideas (Labyrinth has a quest concept in `character-tokens.md`, explicitly marked unresolved) until a real character entry is written for them.
