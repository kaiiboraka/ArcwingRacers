# Ability & Equipment System

Full detail lives in the Obsidian Vault: `ProjectFantasyX/Mechanics & Systems/Abilities/` and `ProjectFantasyX/Mechanics & Systems/Unlockables/`.

---

## Overview

Abilities and equipment are unified. Each **equipment slot** on the character simultaneously represents:
1. A piece of gear that can be upgraded/swapped.
2. An **Ability Slot** — a container for an elemental Orb that determines the spell performed with that slot's action.

Slots are unlocked sequentially through story events — receiving a piece of gear in a story beat IS the mechanical unlock. Before a slot is unlocked, that action type is unavailable. After unlock, the action is available but inert until an Orb is inserted.

## Orbs

Elemental Orbs are gemstones corresponding to each Nature Magic element. Inserting one into a slot grants the matching elemental version of that slot's spell type (e.g., a Fire Orb in Wrist 1 → Fire Projectile spell).

Orbs are found, dropped by enemies, crafted, and rewarded from bosses.

---

## Accessories

**Neck (Amulet/Locket):** Grants elemental resistance. The element is determined by the gemstone in the piece.  
**Finger (Ring/Band):** Grants a passive status effect bonus — either increased potency (damage, slow value, etc.) or increased duration of the status inflicted by that element. Two ring slots allow two different status bonuses simultaneously.
