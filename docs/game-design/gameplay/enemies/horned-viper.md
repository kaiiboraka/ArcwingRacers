# Horned Viper

`[Renamed]` Called "Guardian Viper" in older source material — **Horned Viper** is the current name (the file path is unchanged to avoid breaking links).

An aggressive NPC Viper that defends the Witch's Lair.

## Encountering Horned Vipers

- **At the Swamp Maze entrance:** A single Horned Viper engages the player immediately on entry — see `gameplay/scripted-encounters/enter-witch-cloister.md`.
- **At the final Witch encounter:** Two Horned Vipers are summoned from the Witch's top cauldron as part of the full boss fight — see `gameplay/boss-battles/witch-battle.md`.

## Player Strategies

- **Direct assault:** Only viable with a very powerful Viper — multiple large, aggressive Horned Vipers can converge on the player at once.
- **Captive distraction:** Horned Vipers preferentially go for captives dropped near them inside the lair. Eating a captive pacifies one until it gets hungry again — bringing enough captives for every Horned Viper present lets a player slip past or buy time.
- Horned Vipers have a special affinity for any **loose Captive** on the ground anywhere (not just inside the lair) and will aggro toward it as a distraction until it's eaten — see `gameplay/player-characters/viper-gameplay.md`.

## Combat Exception

Horned Vipers can always bite a player from **action range**, even if the player's Viper is bigger — normal size-based bite rules (only the bigger Viper can bite) don't protect the player here. Once the player closes to **body range**, normal bite rules resume (the bigger Viper can bite as usual). See `gameplay/player-characters/viper-gameplay.md`.

## After Defeat

If left alive when the Witch is defeated, Horned Vipers transform into Castle Guards. Players can then pick them up the same way as a Captive instead of eating them — see `world/locations/swamp-entrance.md` and `game-cycle/endings.md`.
