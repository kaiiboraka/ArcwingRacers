# Hermit

## Role

The Hermit provides hints that explain the game's goals and how to achieve supremacy — for a price. Without the Hermit, the game mechanics and goals are purposefully left ambiguous. Part of the fun is trial, error, and discovery.

## Interaction

- Use the **Bribe button** (Vocal Range) to pay him for advice. Requires having a Thief.
- Attempting to **Bite** the Hermit results in him side-stepping the attack and whopping you on the head for minor damage.
- After **3 failed bite attempts**, the Hermit makes a snide comment. [TBD]
- Visiting the Hermit as a **Grand Viper** triggers the comment: *"Your Majesty..."*

## Hint Tiers

Hints come in 4 categories, increasing in value and cost:

| Tier | Type | Cost | # of Hints |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Beginner | 1 silver coin | 5 |
| 2 | Intermediate | 1 gold coin | 5 |
| 3 | Advanced | 1 gold bar | 3 |
| 4 | Top Secret | 1 goblet | 2 — "Hint for beating Witch" and "Hint for beating Captain" |

- Higher tiers reveal more valuable information (basic concepts → how to win). `[TBD]` The specific wording of all hints besides the two Tier 4 topics above isn't written yet.

One T1-flavored example line survives in source material `[Source: iphone-notes.txt]`:

> "How'd that saying go? Grab the bull by the... no. Grab the Viper by the Tail! Yes, that's it!"
- You start with all T1 hints unlocked.
- If you can't afford the next tier, the Hermit jokes about inflation and increasing costs.
- If you're destitute (coin = minimum purse), he gives you **3 silver coins** and a comment instead of a hint, so you're never completely stuck. T1 hints can all be earned for free this way.

## First Meeting Foreshadowing

- First line: *"Greetings, my fellow traveler!"*
- Later: *"Don't worry, it'll all make sense. I'm a professional."* [Ref: Bill & Ted's Excellent Adventure (1989)]
