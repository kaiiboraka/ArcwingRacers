# King

The King has **no game mechanics by design** — he is not a fightable, interactive, or AI-driven NPC. He exists only as a character in two **scripted encounters**:

1. A brief scripted appearance shortly before the player fights the **Captain of the Guard** (the final Castle boss — see `gameplay/boss-battles/captain-battle.md`): the King spots the player, scampers inside, and the gate slams behind him, before the Captain himself drops down to fight.
2. A longer scripted story sequence that plays after the Captain of the Guard is defeated. There are many variations of the first part of this sequence where you first walk in on the king and find the king engaged in some funny/compromising or generally 80s themed situation (see `gameplay/scripted-encounters/enter-kings-palace.md`'s Cameo Pool) before the standard victory scene plays out and the king is toppled from the throne the thief takes over.

Other confirmed facts about the King, from other docs:

- He resides in the Castle and is the Thief's ultimate target — see `world/locations/castle.md` and `gameplay/scripted-encounters/enter-kings-palace.md` (currently a stub).
- **Thief Beats King (primary Thief victory):** as part of the post-boss scripted sequence, the Thief's paired Viper eats the old King; the Thief takes the throne. See `game-cycle/endings.md`.
- **Viper Beats King (optional Viper boss encounter):** the new King-Thief becomes an ally, opening the castle and periodically sending tribute packages of captives. See `game-cycle/endings.md`.
