## Cinematic Sequence

Credits scroll on the right, lyrics scroll on the left, with a player character animated in between — choreographed tightly to the song's timestamps:

1. **0:13–1:01** — Song opens ("Girl you're my lover and I watch you undercover... Move like a snake hiss sounds you make"). Lyrics establish a "two women, one girl / two faces, two worlds" hidden-identity theme. The Thief character appears on screen partway through (around the "Every time" lyric), hooded, facing away/down.
2. **1:04 — The reveal:** Timed to land exactly on the lyric "...who you are," the Thief looks down toward the camera and pulls off their hood, revealing long red hair with a head shake. `[Notable]` This establishes the default Thief player as visually coded female — a deliberate reveal beat, not a neutral/ambiguous design choice. Homage to Samus [Ref: Metroid (1986)]
3. **1:09–1:14** — She winks, walks toward the lyrics side of the screen, and pauses right at the boundary between the credits half and the lyrics half — staged like a mirror.
4. **1:17 — Transformation:** She crosses through that boundary and turns into a snake exactly as the lyric "Went to the other side" lands. The crossing itself should read as a pixelated disintegration/materialization effect (`[TBD]` exact treatment unspecified).
5. **1:17–1:33** — As a snake, she zips around the lyrics side, then turns back into the Thief exactly on the "one girl" lyric (~1:33).
6. **~1:34–2:30** — Second verse/chorus repeat plays out (mirroring beats 1–2 above, "Two women, one girl... Far far from her heart... No one sees truth in who you are"). At some point in this stretch, **a Hermit cameo** dances onto the credits side doing a funky dance to the beat — including a "robot" move and a moonwalk — then exits. `[TBD]` Exact timestamp for the Hermit bit isn't given in source, only that it falls somewhere in this section.
7. **3:11 — Final stretch:** The credits side disappears; lyrics take over the full screen for the closing lines.
8. **3:18** — The Thief returns, facing the camera, hair flowing, animated zooming from large (~5×) back down to a baseline ~3× size as the final lyrics ("Far from who you are / No one can see truth in who you are") scroll past — the scrolling text fades to sit at half screen height since the oversized Thief occupies the top half.
9. **3:57 — Music and scrolling end.** A message prints out one character at a time (reusing the Hermit dialogue text-printing effect/sound, see `gameplay/npcs/hermit.md`):
   > "To all the incredible, wacky, brilliant, ocd, beautiful, serious, talented, and funny Autistic players: Dream big. You know who you are..."
10. **Closing beat:** The Thief winks, then the camera zooms in on her pixelated face until a single eye fills the screen, cutting to the main menu (or to character selection for the opposite character — `[TBD]` exact transition target unconfirmed).
