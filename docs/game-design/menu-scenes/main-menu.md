# Main Menu

## Elements

### Title Logo
- Words "Vipers & Thieves" with a pixel image of a large Viper looking straight at you, a Thief on its back peeking out one side.
- **Option A:** Viper is part of the logo itself (replacing one or more letters).
- **Option B:** Animation sequence — title words in the foreground, dark shadowy background, Viper emerges from the shadows behind/above the title. A separate source describes this as a "Snake Fade In" — consistent with Option B.

### Music Player
- Accessed via a cassette-tape-with-play-arrow icon on the main menu.
- A song is unlocked the first time you encounter it in-game — when this happens, a little boombox icon flashes in the corner like a save-game notification.
- **Screen:** An old-school boombox; a "V&T Mix Tape" cassette animates into the player. A panel below shows the current track name — tap it to choose from a dropdown of unlocked songs. A colored-bar equalizer animates with the volume.
- **Transport controls** (tap to press each mechanical button down): Stop, Play, Previous, Next, Pause.
- Unplayed tracks get a "New" tag in the player; the main-menu boombox icon also pulses (scales up/down) when there's something new to check out.
- `[TBD]`: whether Viper/Thief theme songs are earned as a random pull from chests, or all available from the start.
- `[Discrepancy]` `town-scenes/beats-alley.md` describes a separate, similarly-themed "interactive boombox for exploring the game soundtrack" as a **town location**. Whether this Main Menu Music Player and the Beats Alley town scene are the same feature accessed two ways, or two distinct/redundant designs, is unresolved — not reconciled here.

### New Game Flow
Starting a new game leads into: Start Game, Purchase Consumables, Purchase Characters, Character Select — i.e., the **Tavern** flow. See `town-scenes/tavern.md`.

### Play Button
- Launches the Intro sequence, followed by the game.
- **[Nice to have]** Hitting Play triggers a brief animation on the logo Viper: eye shimmer, tongue flick, or similar.

### Autism Button
- Autism ribbon icon.
- Opens a panel explaining the company mission to support Autism research, the Autism Mentorship Program (AMP), ThisOrThat app, link to company website, etc.

## Idle Animation Events

If you wait on the title screen, random events fire:

- **Snake Intrusion:** A random snake enters the screen, maybe chasing something.
- **Witch Cackle:** Poof — disappears in smoke.
- **King & His Guards:** March past.
- **Thief:** Sneaking around.
