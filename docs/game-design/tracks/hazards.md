# Track Hazards

## Overview

ArcwingRacers tracks are more than just terrain and obstacles. Systemic hazards create dynamic risk, reward skilled play, and interact with the game's elemental systems. Hazards can be bypassed, mitigated with elemental resists, or used against other racers.

This is a major divergence from EP1R (which mostly has static terrain obstacles).

---

## Hazard Categories

### Elemental / Environmental Hazards

| Hazard | Effect | Counterplay |
|---|---|---|
| Fire vents | Damaging streams of flame, deal damage over time | Time the gap, fire resist mod, shield |
| Lava pools | Instant heavy damage on contact | Stay on path, ice element cools surface |
| Freezing water | Slows pod, reduces traction, can freeze solid | Water resist mod, fire element thaws |
| Poison pools | Damage over time while inside | Avoid, poison resist mod |
| Electricity fields | Stun on contact, chain to nearby racers | Time gap, lightning resist mod |
| Wind tunnels | Push pod off course, can launch into walls | Counter-steer, heavy pod handles better |

### Track Obstacles

| Hazard | Effect | Counterplay |
|---|---|---|
| Webs / leaf thickets | Slow pod down | Destructible with fire abilities |
| Pop-up hazards | Emerge from ground holes unpredictably | Memorize patterns, quick reaction |
| Opening/closing doors | Block path, require side selection | Quick 90° tilt, memorize cycle |
| Swinging spiked balls | Classic death trap on chains | Time the gap |
| Moving blocks | Walls covering parts of track | Speed management, alternate path |
| Falling platforms | Collapse under pod | Keep moving, don't linger |
| Cranes / hoisted crates | Crates suspended on ropes, can swing into path | Observe swing pattern, boost through |

### Wildlife

| Hazard | Behavior | Counterplay |
|---|---|---|
| Wandering livestock | Slow-moving, takes up lane space | Weave around, risk hitting them |
| Bears / ogres / trolls | Actively attack nearby racers | Speed past, abilities to stun |
| Hostile plants | Vines that bite/grab pods near walls | Stay center of track |
| Swooping birds | Dive-bomb from above | Swerve, shield |
| Spiders | Drop down from overhead | Watch for webs above |
| Water creatures | Jump out to bite during water segments | Speed through, expect the timing |
| Mammoth | Stomps on vehicles passing under | Wait for leg lift, speed past |
| Dragon | Tail-swipe flings pods into air, may eat them | Observe tell, quick reaction |
| Pirates | Attack during dock segments | Abilities, speed |

### Environmental Conditions

| Hazard | Effect | Counterplay |
|---|---|---|
| Fog / thick weather | Obscures vision ahead | Learn track layout, follow guard rails |
| Darkness | Limited visibility | Light sources (torches), fire abilities illuminate |
| Earthquakes | Shake screen, pod handling affected | Reduce speed, brace for turns |
| Gas explosions | Random explosive pockets | Audible/visual tells |
| Slippery surfaces | Reduced traction | Lower speed, traction mod, careful steering |

### Civilian Traffic

| Hazard | Effect |
|---|---|
| Carriages with horses | Slow-moving civilian traffic in lanes. Collision damages both pod and carriage. |

### Underwater-Specific

| Hazard | Effect |
|---|---|
| Clams | Snap shut on pods that get too close |
| Marine threats | Various creatures in underwater segments |

### Destructible Environment

| Element | Detail |
|---|---|
| Breakable walls | Boost through to reveal shortcuts or secret areas. |
| Lightable torches | Hit to keep area lit in darkness segments. |

## Hazard Design Principles

1. **Every hazard has a tell.** Whether visual, audible, or based on track position, the player should never feel that a hazard is truly random.
2. **Hazards can be mitigated.** Elemental resist mods, shield, or abilities provide counterplay options.
3. **Hazards create skill expression.** Timed gaps, precise routes, and boost-through-destructibles reward experienced players.
4. **Hazards interact with elements.** Fire melts ice, water conducts lightning, wind blows away water. Elemental Imbalance events can swap which hazards are active.
