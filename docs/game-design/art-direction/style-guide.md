# Art Direction — Style Guide

## Visual Style

**Pixel Art primarily in the early SNES, NES or prior styles.** Possibly with influences from even earlier systems (e.g., all-black backgrounds, ColecoVision era).

The style must be a **conscious choice** — simple is acceptable, but it cannot look like a generic game-maker template. It needs a unique style: possibly a blend of early classic and modern 8-bit art.

## Color Palette

- **Game board background:** Black
- **Image tiles:** Continue with black background
- **Palette:** Modern 8-bit — unlimited palette, more detail and nuanced shading than strict NES limits
- **Character art style reference:** [FFIV style](http://superadventuresingaming.blogspot.com/2013/04/final-fantasy-iv-snes.html)
