# Swamp Maze

The interior level reached after the `gameplay/scripted-encounters/enter-witch-cloister.md` transition — much larger than the small enclosed zone shown for the Witch's area on the world map.

## Layout

Past the entrance Horned Viper (see `gameplay/scripted-encounters/enter-witch-cloister.md`), the level is a maze laced with traps the player must navigate around to reach the Witch.

## Traps

- **Spike Traps:** Deal damage to every Viper body segment that passes over them — a moderate, slow damage drain that continues until your entire tail has cleared the trap.
- **Arrow Traps:** Triggered by a pressure plate. If you don't dodge in time, the arrow hits your head for severe damage. If your head successfully dodges, the arrow flies harmlessly over any body/tail segments still crossing the plate.

## Enemies

Possibly a couple of Horned Vipers roaming this outer maze as well, in addition to the one guarding the entrance (see `world/locations/swamp-entrance.md`'s "Approaching the Lair").

## After the Traps

Clearing the trap maze leads to the Witch's Cloister — a second, smaller semi-maze with its own distinct challenges — and the final Witch encounter. See `gameplay/boss-battles/witch-battle.md` for the full sequential flow and the Cloister/boss fight itself.
