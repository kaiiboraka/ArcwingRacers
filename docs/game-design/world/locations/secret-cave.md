# Secret Treasure Cave

## How to Unlock

The cave only appears as a new location on the map for a limited time (**60 seconds** — resolved this session, was "30–60 seconds"; matches the Retrogasm duration in `gameplay/experience-and-levels.md`) after you eat a snake of **exactly the same length as your own Viper** (also the maximum length Viper you can target/eat at all — see `gameplay/player-characters/viper-gameplay.md`'s Eating Vipers section).

`[Discrepancy — flagged, not resolved]` `iphone-notes.txt` describes a different, conflicting unlock condition: looping the map 5 times reveals the cave, with a mossy rock placed at the start that's transformed into the cave entrance by an earthquake after the 5th loop. This doesn't match the Retrogasm-triggered version above — unclear whether this is an earlier/alternate idea that was superseded, a second separate unlock path, or notes for a different cave/secret entirely. Needs your call on which (if either, or both) is canon.

## After the Feat

Upon accomplishing this, you enter a "Retrogasm" state — dizzy with fatigue:

- Your Viper randomly turns out of your control (drunk controls)
- Spinning stars animated above your head
- The area around you goes dark with a neon 3D grid floor
- Your body turns into a Tron-like Light Viper
- A new upbeat, crazy song plays for the duration

## Inside the Cave

If you can stumble your way to find and enter the cave:
- You're presented with **3 chests**. Choose one; the other two flash and disappear.

**Visual details:**
- Neon party lights shining/flashing out of the cave entrance
- Hermit dancing inside?
- Witch going crazy?
- Guard with no helmet?
- Night club inside? Big hair singer rocking out on stage. *(See SNES Shadowrun, Maria Mercurial scene)*

**Speed:** Gets faster and faster, then at the end slows down quickly and stops as you pass out.

## Outcomes

**If you run out of time and fail to find the cave:**
> "What was that all about? Better watch what I eat!"

**If you pass out in the cave after getting the treasure (wake up outside):**
> "What a night!"

**Achievement:** Unlocks the "Retrogasm" achievement.
