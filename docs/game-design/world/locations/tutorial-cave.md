# Tutorial Cave (Starting Cave)

## Overview

The Starting Cave is a learn-on-your-own tutorial area. You must master the basics before you are set free in the world.

After your first playthrough, the exit is available immediately — experienced players can move straight through and exit without engaging the tutorial.

## First Playthrough

- You start as a Viper.
- You are in a small room (slightly larger than the normal screen).
- You're moving to the right, heading toward a wall if you don't turn.
- There is an object below you, so you'll need to turn to investigate.
  - [What is the object? TBD]
- If you pause at the wall for more than 2 seconds, a swipe-down prompt is shown until you act.
- How to introduce hunger? Possible approach: a Thief is in the area and your hunger is draining. Eating the Thief fills the meter completely. Behind the Thief is a chest — your first loot box!
