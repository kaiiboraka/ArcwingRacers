# King's Castle

## Overview

The castle is a fixed special location found on every map. It is the primary goal location for Thief players.

## Spawning Thieves

New Thieves are emitted regularly from the King's Castle, populating the map with competition.

## Visiting (At the Gates)

- **Bribe your way in:** Pay a very large bribe to the Castle Gate Guards for entry.
- **Purchase a captive:** Buy a captive (snake food) at the gates. Captives can ride on your Viper behind your Thief, or be eaten for food. You can have multiple captives on your Viper, each positioned behind the Thief, down the line — up to the Viper's full length.

## Defeating the King

The castle is **inaccessible to all but the richest Thieves** (large gate bribe required). Inside the courtyard, a small horde of soldiers awaits in the corners, ready to slay any would-be thief. The soldiers deal damage to the hunger/health bar.

- Only a powerful Viper ally gives you a real chance of fighting through to the palace.
- Once there: the Viper eats the old King, and the Thief takes the throne and crown.
- **If playing as a Thief:** This is the primary victory condition. Triggers the Ending sequence → New Game+ as Grand Viper.
- **If playing as a Viper:** The new King ally periodically sends tribute packages of captives. A notification appears pointing you to the castle ~5 seconds before tribute arrives (grinning King, thumbs up, captive in chat bubble). No new Thieves spawn from the castle after this — only tribute captives.

## Castle Gate Guards

- 2 guards at the gates, patrolling a set of guard post waypoints next to the gate.
- Eating one causes the other to pursue and attack. Guards give up the chase if they travel too far from the castle.
- Eating both triggers a squad of soldiers dispatched to hunt you — they do not give up.
- Guards respawn back to capacity over time.

**Reinforcement timing:**
- If a guard is eaten, a replacement is sent out after **1 minute**, and the surviving guard aggros and attacks the player.
- If both guards end up eaten: the reinforcement timer resets and **both** guards come back out together. In addition, a squad of soldiers is dispatched from the castle to patrol the region for **5 minutes** — if the player defeats that patrol, no further action is taken (it doesn't escalate further, unlike the chase-to-the-death squad triggered by eating both gate guards described above). `[TBD]` Whether this patrol squad is the same trigger as "eating both gate guards" above, or a related-but-distinct escalation tier, isn't fully disambiguated in source material.
