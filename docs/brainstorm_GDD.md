# ArcwingRacers
High-speed low-poly racing game.

> LIKE POD RACER... ~~IN SPACE~~ IN ELYTHIA

## Terminology Brainstorm

Arcwings — Arc for arcane. Blade or Rig are good too. Arc-rigs maybe?

- Chariot/Cockpit: **Blade**
- Engines: **Wings**
- Whole pod: **Arcwing**
- Cross beams: **Arc-rig**
- Pilots: **BLADE RIDERS**, or **Arc Riders**

"Wings" are like Solar Sails (comprised of energy cells) attached to magical machinery. Inspiration from airships across Final Fantasy, Treasure Planet, Stardust etc.
Blade body's overall shape (on average) like a reverse of a Naboo Starfighter, where the blade comes to a long point. Not a hard rule, just a standard design basis.

## Primary Differences from EP1R (Brainstorm)

Every character gets a weapon or ability of some kind, instead of just Sebulba.

A way to earn money outside of racing and trading. Random "Mercenary" Race — jump into a random race on a random track with random opponents and get paid based on your performance. Perhaps tying winnings allocation to difficulty.

More complexity in itemization systems.
More ways to play with Random / procedural / roguelike mode.
More diversity in enemy AI experience — randomized loadouts instead of flat speed values.
More environmental hazards and world mechanics.

### MAYBE (Goofy / Bonus Modes)

- Big Head / Little Head mode, Halo Birthday Party style
- Mario Kart party mode with items
- Alternate objective modes: Teams, Capture the Flag, Rocket League, Halo Zombies, City Trial / Kirby Air Ride

## Mechanics Brainstorm

### Racing
- Copy the floaty pod feel
- Nose-tipping mechanic for air control
- COPY THE BOOST MECHANIC EXACTLY — over-abuse catches you on fire
- Base stats from Ep 1 Racer as baseline
- Repair is great because of itemization
- Time system — best lap/overall records per character and overall

### Level Design
- Environmental hazards, temperature awareness
- Boost overheats you (death in hot areas, salvation in cold)
- Boost lowers turn response — ice already slippery
- Obstacles, monsters, weird things, underwater
- Levels split: geometry/terrain + features/hazards on top
- **Elemental Imbalance** — Mario Galaxy prankster comets style
- Jumps and skill-based shortcuts (not catch-up mechanics)
- Secret tunnels through destroyable walls
- Off-road paths you boost across
- Underground lava caves

### Characters & Elemental Abilities

#### Character-Agnostic System
Weapon type × element plug-and-play. "Flamethrower" vs "Breath of Frost/Cone of Cold." "Seed Bomb" vs "Shrapnel Grenade."

#### Character-Specific Abilities (Early Brainstorm)
- Fire: Afterburner — ACTIVE, next boost destroys other pods, no slowdown on impact (BULLET BILL)
- Iron: Fortress — ACTIVE, temporarily immune to damage (Super star)
- Water: Tidal wave — ACTIVE, knock away surrounding racers
- Lightning: Electron Charge — ACTIVE, pass harmlessly through racers/hazards during boost
- Earth: ?
- Wood: ?
- Ice: ?
- Wind: ?

#### Unlockable Characters
Progress through story/tournaments unlocks characters (PER RACER LICENSE / file). SECRET characters / bosses have unique permanent pod parts that cannot be changed. Some (Archons) get permanent passive versions of active abilities.

### Pod Modularity Brainstorm
Returning upgradeable part system, now with:
- Slots for mod components with elemental affinity
- Effects: Environmental resist, Stat kiss-curse, Perks
- BIS equipment has elemental affinities — tradeoffs at highest level
- "Equipment Quality/Rarity" — not explicit, implicit in stat rank. No finding same item at multiple quality levels.

### Economy Brainstorm
- Junkyard + item progression as baseline
- Make money outside races by trading/repairing parts
- Repair components with purchasable (upgradeable?) helpers
- Store and keep parts + direct selling (not just trade-in)
- Progression tied to License Rank, not race count
- Difficulty selection controls winnings allocation
- Elemental mod parts: special currency (cores), nation shops

### Archon Races Brainstorm
- Upper echelon. Playable after unlocking Archon character.
- Choose Archon, choose region, race through area's tracks.
- Elemental advantages at play.
- Roguelike progression: choose between Rest/Repair, Parts Shop, Race
- Race types: Normal, Ring-Race, Quick-Run, Last Racer Running, Hyper-hazard
- Race until you crash (or 3 strikes). Elimination style.
- Elemental Imbalance happens frequently.
- Slay the Spire style map progression.
- Rewards: Elemental mod cores?

### Multiplayer Brainstorm
- Singleplayer against self ghost or others

## Aesthetic Design Brainstorm

- Low-poly 3D. Simple textures. Immersive UI.
- Charming character models with expressive quirky animations (Male Fire racer is Maui)
- Punch-Out stereotypes from each nation, one per country. 2 each (M/F). Main cast as secret/boss characters.
- At least one track in each country for biome diversity
- Speed feel: tiling/stretched textures + actual speed = natural motion blur
- UI: Speed gauge. Health as in-universe semi-diegetic approximation of the pod.
