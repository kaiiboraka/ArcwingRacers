## Acquisition

**Getting Featured**
- Submit for Apple's "promote your app" consideration.
- PR/marketing spend blitz concentrated in a 2-3 day window to accelerate downloads, ratings, and organic rankings.
- Use new Apple features; optimize for the latest Apple phone.
- Network for Apple/App-Store-reviewer contacts via LinkedIn outreach — short pitch on the unique company/game, ask for a look and feedback on what it'd take to get featured, mention the studio's autism mission.

**Organic App Search Rankings**
- Test/optimize ASO content (icon, description, keywords).
- Fight for high-volume, high-score reviews.
- Split-test gameplay balance factors and rewards to optimize key metrics:
  - **Engagement** — `[Design-adjacent: not yet reflected in game-design/ — see note below]`
    - **Daily Quests:** achievement-like, reset daily, bonus rewards for completion, with a completion timer; completed quests get replaced with new ones. Target ~30 minutes of play/day to clear them all. Examples: "Play 5/10/15 Thieves", "Obtain a Level 8/10/12/15 Viper".
    - **Weekly Goal:** a large, valuable reward for completing a large quantity of content (many hours of play, or a lot of consumable IAP, etc.), on a week-long countdown timer. `[TBD]` Possibly break for 1-2 weeks between events to make them feel special — flagged as untested in source.
    - **Notifications:** event started, daily quests available, free chest available. `[TBD]` Time-of-day and player segmentation untested.
  - **Retention:**
    - **Daily Activity Bonus:** increases for consecutive days played; show the specific (not random) reward for returning tomorrow.
    - **Subscription product:** "30 days of Treasure" — open 2 free chests + a currency amount per day. `[Cross-ref: see monetization/iap.md's VIP Subscription Pricing Reference for the resolved, concrete version of this — 1,500 Gems/month at 50/day plus a bonus T3 Royal Chest monthly. Numbers don't match exactly; treat the iap.md version as the more current one.]`
    - News releases teasing upcoming content, possibly behind an unlock timer.
  - **Bounce Rate:** simplify the first session — get players into the action as smoothly as possible; eliminate barriers/news popups, possibly skip straight to gameplay.

**Paid App Search:** Test top target keywords with small budgets; once a winner is found, push a marketing blitz timed with a major PR event.

**Facebook Paid Ads:** Lookalike campaigns.

**Paid Video Ads:** Unity Ads.

**Social Media**
- Engage on Facebook/Twitter: announce events, IAP discounts, ask for feedback.
- Autism social media events — dedicated hashtag, call for participation.
- **"Vipers vs. Thieves" team event idea** `[Design-adjacent: not yet reflected in game-design/ — see note below]`: pick Team Viper or Team Thief; top 50 participants on the winning team get a prize. Alternate framing: a 1-week in-game event where all your runs play as your chosen team's character type, racking up points for that team — winning team unlocks a new character for free, losing team gets a purchase offer for it instead.
- Ask followers to: rate the app, hit a follower-count goal, invite friends, share gameplay stories, make YouTube videos, donate to the autism cause — with merch rewards (e.g., 50 t-shirt giveaways) for participation.
  - **T-shirt design idea** `[Source: iphone-notes.txt, dated 4/25]`: Front — "Are you a Viper", Back — "...or a Thief?", with character pixel art included.
  - **Second t-shirt design idea** `[Source: iphone-notes.txt]`: Front — "Games for Autism", Back — Vipers & Thieves logo.
  - **"Ask Me Anything" social contest idea** `[Source: iphone-notes.txt]`: Ask entrants to read the studio's mission page and check out Vipers & Thieves, then tell the studio what they'd say if someone asked them about (1) Games for Autism and (2) Vipers & Thieves. X winners (count `[TBD]`) get a premium t-shirt — pitched with the hook that wearing it might prompt someone to actually ask them that question for real.
- Post video content: trailers and gameplay highlights (especially new features).
- Post media coverage links and ask for retweets; post about related industry events/games the studio likes.

**In-Game Social Media:** Link to social pages from the main menu — wait until there's enough content/fanbase before adding this.

**YouTube:** Post all trailers/gameplay footage; pitch reviewers/streamers/Let's-Players; ask the fanbase to make their own videos.

**Backlinks**
- Link to App Store pages, the website's game page, or the social media game page (App Store preferred).
- Medium: blog posts (own blog), blog/forum comments, social media comments, signature links.
- Sources: competitor game fan sites/forums/channels, general game discussion (e.g., Reddit), indie dev channels, autism user/gamer groups and forums.
